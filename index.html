About Me

Turning curiosity about AI into practical skills, confidence, and real-world capability

I'm a Data & AI professional with over 8 years of experience working across AI implementation, data-driven solutions and advanced analytics. During my time at EY, I worked with financial institutions and large organisations, helping them use AI, machine learning and cloud technologies to improve the way they operate and make better decisions.

After EY, I made a deliberate move into coaching because I wanted to help people who were genuinely excited about AI but didn't always know where to start. I wanted to make the field feel more accessible and help people turn their interest in AI into practical skills, confidence and a genuine career.

At Multiverse, I've had the opportunity to coach over 150 Data & AI apprentices across the UK, working with learners from a wide range of organisations. I've supported them across areas including LLMs, AI agents, machine learning pipelines, responsible AI and real-world applications of Data & AI.

I've also supported 140+ learners through to the End Point Assessment stage, achieving a 95%+ gateway pass rate. This has included running independent EPA practice assessments, carrying out readiness checks, and giving practical, action-focused feedback on project work and portfolio-based professional discussions, helping learners understand how to demonstrate the relevant Knowledge, Skills and Behaviours (KSBs).

What motivates me most is seeing someone go from being curious about AI to feeling confident enough to apply it in the workplace. That's ultimately why I moved from consulting into coaching — to help more people get started, build real capability and see what they can achieve in Data & AI.

My technical expertise spans Python, machine learning, Generative AI, Large Language Models, SQL, graph databases and cloud technologies. This combination of industry experience and coaching allows me to connect technical concepts with real-world application and help learners develop genuine workplace capability.
